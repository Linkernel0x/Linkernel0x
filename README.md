### ⚡ Fun Facts about me and tech

* 🎒 **School's IT baby boss:** I've loved tech since childhood. In primary school, my teachers used to let me skip PE (which I absolutely despised) so they could take me around the school premises to troubleshoot and fix computer infrastructure issues.

* 🖨️ **Printer Allegations:** During primary school, I accidentally permanent-bricked/soft-locked a network printer by completely stripping access privileges for both the `Everyone` and `Administrators` groups. It has since spent its life serving as an expensive paperweight and a makeshift plant stand. Oops. `0-0`

* 🗺️ **Virtual CEO:** In middle school, teachers frequently forgot I was even present in class. I used to sit silently in front of the classroom PC, designing a full 3D model of the school building and configuring a mock corporate deployment using the school's Google Workspace suite. `>.<` During one of those deep dives, I stumbled across the word `Python`, and everything changed.

* 💣 **Crime and ~~Punishment~~ *Redemption*:** In my freshman year of high school, I wrote a fork bomb named *"don't even try to click me"* to prank a friend, but accidentally left it active on a school station. Terrified, when I realised, I rushed back post-school and waited 4 full hours on the stairwell for a janitor or a teacher to arrive just so I could delete it. Luckily, it never executed.

* 🗓️ **The Schedule Dossier:** I wrote other silly stuff such as a tiny script meant to display a Windows alert saying 'Unexpected error! Windows worked as intended.' during Chrome's opening procedure. During those years, I also built a small CLI tool to generate oral exam calendars and randomize the student lineup (obviously giving my friends a little help ^^), though we never actually used it. Since then I've stopped using technology in a harmful way!
